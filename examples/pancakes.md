# Grandma's Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1 ½ cups flour
- 3 ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 ¼ cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp melted butter

## Method

1. Sift together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
2. Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg and butter. Mix until smooth.
3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium-high heat.
4. Pour ¼ cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form, then flip.
5. Brown the other side and serve hot.
